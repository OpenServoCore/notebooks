# Stage 0. Instruments

The rulers the other stages grade against. Each gets an error bar and a
clock offset here before it measures anything. No board and no servo on the
bench, so this sitting needs only the host, the DPS and the DMM, plus
the camera and the thermocouple for their own experiments.

Each experiment is a folder here with its `capture.py`, its notebook and
the `results.json` the notebook writes. With no servo and no board, the
datasets land in `telemetry/instruments/<experiment>/capture-N/`.

## TL;DR

The short version, for a reader who wants the numbers before the
notebooks. The table is filled from each experiment's `results.json`, so it
says what the notebooks wrote. Each bound says what it is. A spread or a
standard error is statistical, a range is the edges of what was seen, and a
class is the instrument's manual.

### Key findings

- The DMM's log can be placed on the host clock as is, with no drift over
  minutes. Its packets come about every half second, so nothing read
  through it is placed in time closer than about a second.
- A current that has to be known to a percent is read as millivolts across
  a known shunt. The 20 A range's class is twenty times looser than the
  millivolt range's.
- The three loads are fixed resistances inside the power range measured.
  Use the driven cold values, since the two-wire ohms reading sits about
  50 mohm low on a 5 ohm part.
- The thermocouple reads within about 0.5 C over 0 to 100 C with no
  correction, and differences to a few tenths, once about 30 s have passed
  in air since the last change at the tip.
- The camera tag resolves about a hundredth of a degree frame to frame, and
  its error over a turn follows the plate's tilt, not the lens. The STAT
  lamp clapper places a frame against the host log to within two
  milliseconds rms.

### Open questions

- DMM function ([dmm](dmm/dmm.ipynb), [thermocouple](thermocouple/thermocouple.ipynb)).
  Whether the lag changes with the DMM's function, and the packet rate on
  ohms. On temperature the packet interval is the same as on DC V. A repeat
  of the dmm capture on ohms and on temperature would say.
- Load inductance ([power-resistors](power-resistors/power-resistors.ipynb)).
  Only quoted from the part class. A known step read by the board's own
  current sense in stage 1 would give it.
- Load temperature at the top step ([power-resistors](power-resistors/power-resistors.ipynb)).
  A thermocouple on the case during a repeat would say how long a stage 1
  run can hold that power.
- Tag coplanarity ([camera-tag](camera-tag/camera-tag.ipynb)). Whether the
  cardboard holds the two tags in one plane. The same turn on a rigid plate
  would separate a bowed plate from the mapping's own sensitivity.
- A reference with more corners ([camera-tag](camera-tag/camera-tag.ipynb)).
  A small ChArUco patch as the reference would show how much of the error
  is the four-corner mapping.
- Motion blur ([camera-tag](camera-tag/camera-tag.ipynb)). Nothing here
  covers a horn moving at hundreds of degrees per second. A turn by a motor
  at a known rate, or stage 3 with the pot as the second witness, would
  show it.
- Light ([camera-tag](camera-tag/camera-tag.ipynb)). The still repeated in
  dim light would show how the noise floor moves with sensor noise.
- Extraction speed ([camera-tag](camera-tag/camera-tag.ipynb)). Searching
  near the previous frame's tags would be several times faster. Not needed
  yet.
- Thermocouple slope ([thermocouple](thermocouple/thermocouple.ipynb)).
  Whether the 0.7 C at the boil belongs to the thermocouple or to the
  shallow bath. A deeper bath would say. Not planned, since the accuracy is
  already enough for stage 3.
- Lamp onset ([clock-sync](clock-sync/clock-sync.ipynb)). Whether a flash
  starts at the first lit frame or the first fully lit one. A threshold
  crossing may halve the scatter.
- Dropped frames ([clock-sync](clock-sync/clock-sync.ipynb)). Why the phone
  drops frames, and whether a longer or brighter clip drops more. Every
  stage 3 clip carries its own flashes and will show.

### What the next stage takes from here

Stage 1 grades the board against these instruments.

- [current-scale](../1-board/current-scale/current-scale.ipynb) reads the
  bare and series loads' cold values and the leads and ammeter in series
  with them, plus the DMM's lag and its 20 A class.
- [terminal-chain](../1-board/terminal-chain/terminal-chain.ipynb) reads
  the bare load's cold value, the DMM's lag and packet interval, and its
  22 V class.
- [ntc-curve](../1-board/ntc-curve/ntc-curve.ipynb) corrects every
  thermocouple reading by the offset and slope, t = (reading - offset) /
  slope, shifts the DMM log by its lag, and grades the curve with the DMM's
  temperature class.
- [opamp-temperature](../1-board/opamp-temperature/opamp-temperature.ipynb)
  reads the DMM's temperature, 20 A and 220 mV classes.
- [bridge-states](../1-board/bridge-states/bridge-states.ipynb) reads the
  bare load's cold value.
- rest-noise and sample-timing read nothing from here.

Stage 2 reads only the DMM and the thermocouple.

- [rest-resistance](../2-motor/rest-resistance/rest-resistance.ipynb)
  corrects its thermocouple readings the same way and bounds its ohms
  readings by the DMM's 220 ohm class and step.
- [bemf-coast](../2-motor/bemf-coast/bemf-coast.ipynb) reads the DMM's
  220 mA class, for the meter in series with one motor lead.
- [teardown](../2-motor/teardown/teardown.ipynb) corrects its thermocouple
  readings the same way and quotes the settle time in air.
- inductance, no-load-current and speed-torque read nothing from here.

The camera-tag and clock-sync results wait for stage 3. The pot experiments
there read the camera tag's error, and every clip there carries its own
dozen flashes, fit the way clock-sync fits them.

## [dmm](dmm/dmm.ipynb)

Targets: the lag of the DMM's log against the host clock, and its spread.
Lands in the DMM's registry entry.

Needs: DMM, DPS, host.

How: the host steps the DPS voltage at logged times while the DMM logs
DC volts. The step edges in the DMM log against the host times give the
lag. The reading accuracy comes from the datasheet and is written into the
result, not measured.

Run: `uv run python stages/0-instruments/dmm/capture.py`, then `dmm/dmm.ipynb`.

Result: `dmm/results.json`.

## [power-resistors](power-resistors/power-resistors.ipynb)

Targets: the value of each load and its drift with power. Lands in the
loads' registry entries, which stage 1 reads.

Needs: DMM, DPS, loads, host.

How: one run with no load and the DMM on DC A carrying the DPS current, for
the lead and ammeter resistance. Then one run per load: ohms at rest from the
DMM, then the DPS current through the DMM and the load with the DPS's readback
for the voltage. Resistance against power gives the self-heating slope.

Run: `uv run python stages/0-instruments/power-resistors/capture.py <bare|cap|series|leads>`
for each, then `power-resistors/power-resistors.ipynb`.

Result: `power-resistors/results.json`.

## [camera-tag](camera-tag/camera-tag.ipynb)

Targets: the angle error and the noise floor of the tag pipeline on the
real bench. Lands in the camera's registry entry, which the pot experiments
in stage 3 read.

Needs: camera (any recent iPhone, filming 1080p slow motion at 240 fps, with
the settings in the notebook's Procedure), an overhead mount holding it about
20 cm above the desk, a plate to glue the tags to, host.

How: two tags printed on one sheet, so their relative angle is fixed by the
print, glued to a plate. The lens is calibrated from a clip of the flat
board filmed at the working distance, then the plate is turned by hand
through a full turn in steps and filmed from above at that same distance.
The relative angle has to come out the same at every step, and a still of
the plate gives the frame to frame noise.

Run: `uv run python stages/0-instruments/camera-tag/capture.py`, which prints the sheets
and waits for the clips, then `camera-tag/camera-tag.ipynb`.

Result: `camera-tag/results.json`.

## [thermocouple](thermocouple/thermocouple.ipynb)

Targets: the thermocouple's offset, slope and time constant. Lands in the thermocouple's
registry entry, which the thermal experiments in stage 3 read.

Needs: thermocouple, DMM, an ice bath and boiling water.

How: the ice bath gives the zero, boiling water corrected for altitude gives
the slope, and dipping the thermocouple from air into the bath gives the time
constant.

Run: `uv run python stages/0-instruments/thermocouple/capture.py`, then
`thermocouple/thermocouple.ipynb`.

Result: `thermocouple/results.json`.

## [clock-sync](clock-sync/clock-sync.ipynb)

Targets: the offset and drift of the camera's clock against the host log,
and how late the lamp follows the host's write. Lands in the camera's
registry entry. The DMM's own lag against the host is the dmm experiment.

Needs: clapper (a servo board on the bus, with a firmware that has
`stat_flash_ticks`), the `osc` tool, camera, host.

How: `osc clapper` flashes the servo's STAT lamp a dozen times at irregular
gaps, logging the host time either side of each write, while the phone
films the lamp. The lamp's level per frame comes out of the clip in the
extraction; the flash onsets against the logged writes give offset and
rate by one straight-line fit.

Run: `uv run python stages/0-instruments/clock-sync/capture.py`, then
`clock-sync/clock-sync.ipynb`.

Result: `clock-sync/results.json`.
